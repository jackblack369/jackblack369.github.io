Reading the table

  • Score — BenchLM’s BenchAlign composite across their benchmark suite. BenchLM never interpolates missing scores, so a model’s score can be lower-confidence without being wrong.
  • Output $/1M — the provider’s list price per million output tokens. Input and cached prices are usually lower; check the model page before budgeting.
  • Context — maximum context window. M is a million tokens.
  • — — the provider does not publish a list price (typically self-hosted or open weights).
